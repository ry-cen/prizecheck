## Question

Can the application safely and maintainably leverage Limitless TCG to group every English Card Printing that counts under the same rules-relevant card name, improve variant or reprint coverage, or later map names across languages? Determine exactly what its card pages and “other printings” relationships mean; whether a documented API, downloadable dataset, stable endpoint, or permission for automated ingestion exists; its identifiers, coverage, provenance, terms, robots/rate constraints, update behavior, and failure modes; and whether it adds anything the English-only MVP cannot derive from TCGdex exact names. Distinguish a supported integration from scraping a human-facing website.
