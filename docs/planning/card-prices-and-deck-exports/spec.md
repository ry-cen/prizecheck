# Card prices and Deck exports

**Status:** implementation in progress on `dev`; testing seams confirmed

**Label:** ready-for-agent

## Problem Statement

Collectors need to see a defensible USD market estimate for the exact physical Card Editions in their catalog, Holdings, private Decks, and Published Decks. They also need complete exports to Pokémon TCG Live and TCGplayer Mass Entry without silently substituting a Printing, finish, product, quantity, or private Deck state.

The current application correctly treats a Card Edition as a physical variant of one Card Printing, and a Holding as a count of that Edition in one Storage Leaf. It does not yet provide pricing, refresh behavior, export formatting, or a public-safe mechanism for mutable quotes and verified metadata on fixed Published Decks. Bounded source and reference observations do not prove universal catalog support, exact physical-product matching, destination acceptance, or deployed behavior.

## Solution

Add price, Estimated Deck Cost, Pricing Coverage, and export experiences built on exact Card Edition identity. Use USD TCGplayer market quotes supplied through TCGdex only when a reviewed Marketplace Mapping establishes the same physical Edition and quote variant. Show quote age and uncertainty honestly, retain defensible cached data on failed refresh, and never represent missing or invalidated quotes as zero.

Refresh is automatic and access-triggered. Private and public Deck costs use every included quantity, independent of pagination. Live exports use reviewed deterministic identifiers; TCGplayer uses a product-ID Mass Entry link only when every card has a verified product mapping, otherwise complete text. Published Decks retain frozen card content, quantities, and display fields while public-safe metadata and quotes update for the same opaque public Edition reference.

Implementation completion, target-datastore correction preflight, and launch validation are separate gates. This specification defines behavior and acceptance intent; it does not claim that any of those gates has passed.

## User Stories

1. As a collector, I want to see a USD market price for an exact Card Edition, so that a normal, reverse, holo, or other physical variant is not priced as another finish.
2. As a collector, I want the card information view to show a quote timestamp or an explicit unknown-age state, so that I can assess its freshness.
3. As a collector, I want catalog and Holding rows that render a price to count as accessed, so that stale information can refresh without a separate action.
4. As a collector, I want an Unspecified Variant to remain unpriced when its physical finish cannot be proved, so that uncertainty is not converted into a misleading value.
5. As a collector, I want a missing quote to say unavailable rather than $0, so that absence does not look like a valuation.
6. As a collector, I want the same defensible quote to be shared across my relevant views, so that repeated views do not create duplicate source work.
7. As a collector, I want an Estimated Deck Cost to multiply each exact Edition quote by its quantity, so that my Deck total reflects what is physically in the Deck.
8. As a collector, I want Pricing Coverage to count priced copies over every copy in the Deck, so that a partial subtotal is not mistaken for a complete value.
9. As a collector, I want a six-copy example with three $0.25 copies, two stale $0.43 copies, and one unpriced copy to show a $1.61 subtotal and 5/6 priced, so that subtotal and coverage behavior is concrete.
10. As a collector, I want stale quote counts and unknown-age quote counts shown with a Deck total, so that mixed-freshness totals are not presented as uniformly current.
11. As a collector, I want a quote whose provider timestamp is over 48 hours old labeled stale, so that source age remains visible after a successful cache check.
12. As a collector, I want a successful source check to refresh check age without changing a provider quote's age, so that an old marketplace quote is not falsely presented as new.
13. As a collector, I want a confirmed unavailable Edition cached for one hour, so that repeat visits do not repeatedly request a known absence.
14. As a collector, I want a later failed retry to retain a confirmed unavailable result, so that an older quote cannot reappear after confirmed absence.
15. As a collector, I want partial provider successes to update independently, so that one unavailable or failed Edition does not discard other defensible prices.
16. As a collector, I want cached results used when the last successful validated check is less than one hour old, so that ordinary reads are fast and bounded.
17. As a collector, I want cached quotes shown when their successful-check age is at least one hour but under 24 hours while eligible work refreshes in the background, so that the page remains useful.
18. As a collector, I want only price and Deck-cost displays to wait at hard expiry, so that non-price page content remains responsive.
19. As a collector, I want the complete price interaction to stop waiting after three seconds, so that a large Deck does not receive three seconds per card.
20. As a collector, I want failed or timed-out work to show a labeled cached fallback or unavailable state, so that the interface does not imply a fresh source quote.
21. As a collector, I want no Refresh prices button or force-refresh endpoint, so that no user bypasses the shared cache expiry or backoff.
22. As a collector, I want following a link not to prefetch prices for every destination, so that unviewed catalog entries do not cause a sweep.
23. As a collector, I want a private Deck cost to read one consistent complete quantity set, so that pagination and concurrent inventory changes cannot silently omit copies.
24. As a collector, I want to copy complete Pokémon TCG Live text for a Deck, so that every included copy is represented.
25. As a collector, I want Live rows combined only when verified derived identifiers match, so that same-name Printings are not incorrectly merged.
26. As a collector, I want category, set, collector, and rule transformations derived from reviewed data, so that Live identifiers do not depend on guessed per-card mappings.
27. As a collector, I want a complete Live export blocked and its unsupported cards identified, so that no card is silently dropped or substituted.
28. As a collector, I want an unknown physical finish to remain eligible for Live only when its Printing has defensible Live data, so that game representation and marketplace pricing remain independent.
29. As a collector, I want to open a TCGplayer Mass Entry product link only when every card has a verified mapping, so that a purchase link is never silently partial.
30. As a collector, I want complete selectable Mass Entry text whenever a product mapping is missing or a link is unusable or too large, so that I retain a full manual fallback.
31. As a collector, I want copy success reported only after the clipboard write succeeds, so that a failed clipboard permission is not misreported as copied.
32. As a collector, I want empty Deck exports disabled with a clear explanation, so that no empty destination action is presented as meaningful.
33. As a collector, I want incomplete, non-60-card, and unusually large Decks to preserve every quantity and show their applicable warnings or text fallback, so that no quantity is capped, split, or hidden.
34. As a collector, I want to report missing or incorrect export metadata, so that a catalog maintainer can review shared corrections.
35. As a catalog maintainer, I want reports to remain unapproved until review, so that a Deck owner cannot establish a personal price or export mapping.
36. As a catalog maintainer, I want reviewed evidence, reviewer, date, and source/rule revision retained, so that corrections have provenance and precedence.
37. As a collector, I want a changed or conflicting Marketplace Mapping to quarantine its quote and product export immediately, so that cached data cannot bypass invalidation.
38. As a collector, I want valid Live eligibility to remain independent of a Marketplace Mapping conflict, so that one target's uncertainty does not disable the other target without cause.
39. As a collector, I want manual Catalog Overrides and retired source records to retain their reviewed meaning, so that correction work does not erase established identity evidence.
40. As a Published Deck viewer, I want prices and exports to use the published card rows and quantities, so that private Deck edits never change a public result.
41. As a Published Deck viewer, I want current verified metadata and quotes to apply to the same fixed published Edition, so that published facts can refresh without changing the published card.
42. As a Published Deck owner, I want republish to capture a new fixed Deck snapshot, so that I control when changed private contents become public.
43. As a Published Deck viewer, I want a revoked token to remain unavailable even if delayed refresh work completes, so that revocation cannot be undone by enrichment.
44. As a public visitor, I want price access to use only public-safe projections even when I have cookies, so that the request never opens a session or PostgreSQL connection.
45. As a public visitor, I want the public page's non-price content to remain available during provider delay or failure, so that a price refresh does not block the snapshot.
46. As a public visitor, I want totals calculated from every frozen published quantity and eligible shared Edition quote, so that pricing coverage reflects the public Deck rather than the currently loaded rows.
47. As a public visitor, I want a stale public response discarded after republish, so that a quote or export cannot attach to newer frozen content.
48. As a public visitor, I want Listed and Unlisted discovery behavior unchanged, so that prices do not add search fetching, cost sorting, or cost filtering.
49. As a viewer of a legacy Published Deck, I want a price or precise export only when frozen evidence proves the same Printing and Edition, so that historical ambiguity is not guessed away.
50. As a viewer of a legacy Published Deck with a proven Printing but unknown finish, I want Live eligibility considered independently while price/product eligibility remains unavailable, so that the two targets remain honest.
51. As a viewer of an unprovable legacy row, I want the page to remain readable with existing export fallbacks, so that uncertainty does not erase the public snapshot.
52. As an operator, I want shared automatic work fenced, coalesced, and backed off, so that concurrent private and public visits do not produce duplicate or stale writes.
53. As an operator, I want public publication content, visibility, and discovery revision insulated from quote updates, so that normal refreshes do not churn public search cursors.
54. As an operator, I want durable recovery to replay frozen public content and opaque references while preserving newer invalidation, so that recovery cannot resurrect a token or obsolete quote state.
55. As an owner preparing launch, I want support labels tied to actual destination, physical-match, and walkthrough evidence, so that bounded fixtures are not presented as universal proof.

## Implementation Decisions

- Preserve the existing domain model: a Card Edition is a physical variant of one Card Printing; a Holding is a positive integer quantity of one Edition in one Storage Leaf. Quantities are serialized as base-10 integer strings, and existing inventory limits remain in force. Pricing and export inputs must not redefine Edition identity, reassign Holdings, or merge quantities.
- Introduce a shared pricing/quote contract that accepts a reviewed Edition and mapping revision, returns a validated quote, confirmed unavailability, or a labeled unavailable/failure state, and preserves provider quote timestamp separately from successful-check timestamp. Amount arithmetic uses exact decimals; externally represented quantities remain decimal strings.
- Surface exact Edition prices in card information, catalog/Holding rows, private Decks, and Published Decks. Estimated Deck Cost is a copy-weighted subtotal, excludes shipping/taxes, and always pairs incomplete totals with Pricing Coverage. An empty Deck shows `$0.00` with “No cards”; a nonempty Deck after a complete scan with no eligible quotes shows “Price unavailable · 0/N cards priced.” An incomplete scan instead shows “Price unavailable; coverage unavailable; N cards total.” Stale means provider quote age strictly over 48 hours, and stale/unknown-age copy counts appear with the aggregate.
- Use automatic access eligibility only. Rendered price surfaces and complete private/Published Deck costs are accesses; links, ownership, publication, and earlier access are not recurring refresh authorization. Enforce the one-hour, 24-hour, and total three-second contract, shared coalescing/backoff, and no user-callable force path.
- Keep confirmed unavailable state separate from fetch failure and protect it from stale quote resurrection. Invalidated Marketplace Mappings also make a quote unavailable until review restores that mapping; Live eligibility remains separate.
- Read a private Deck's complete Edition quantities through one consistent server-side inventory view, independent of browser pagination, before calculating a cost or export.
- Build a deterministic Live-export contract from reviewed Printing/Edition inputs and versioned rules. It returns complete grouped text only when every row is supported; otherwise it identifies all blocking rows. Its layout uses category-count headings, quantity/name/set/number rows, blank lines between groups, and no Total Cards footer. It does not store a per-card Live identifier as source of truth.
- Build a TCGplayer export contract that returns an all-card product-ID link only when all required mappings are verified, otherwise complete selectable text. Text uses verified TCGplayer set and collector specificity where available, falls back to name when it is not, and labels less-specific rows. Before opening Mass Entry, explain that product matching does not guarantee finish or condition and ask the buyer to review destination selections. Clipboard interaction reports success only after the browser confirms the write. Empty, incomplete, non-60, and oversized cases preserve the accepted fallback behavior.
- Separate mutable Marketplace Mapping, quote, and export metadata from physical Variant Descriptor identity. Catalog-maintainer review owns shared corrections, evidence, precedence, conflicts, and invalidation. Reports are inputs to review, not personal overrides. The actual target-datastore preflight remains mandatory before any identity/compatibility correction is selected or executed.
- Extend the immutable public publication envelope with an opaque public Edition reference beside frozen public descriptor/display and quantity. This reference resolves only public-safe shared catalog data, never an internal UUID, owner identity, Holding, session, or private database lookup. Store/recover the reference with the durable publication content operation.
- Use one shared logical quote and coordination store for private and public reads, keyed by Edition and mapping revision. Do not create per-Deck quote copies, persisted Deck totals, or association sidecars. Public reads use a public-safe projection of that shared state.
- Treat the active public token, snapshot version, frozen-content digest, public Edition reference, and current eligibility as authoritative response guards. Revalidate them before a public response; client behavior discards stale-version responses. Publication, republish, owner visibility changes, and revoke alone control active public content and visibility.
- Give public coordination independent serialized/linearizable state from publication/discovery content. Fenced refresh writes may write only their original Edition/mapping-revision result; they cannot alter a publication, visibility, token, or discovery revision. Preserve this ordering through delayed work and recovery.
- Keep anonymous public routes explicitly session-free, including nested price routes with cookies. They use no PostgreSQL wakeup, private inventory read, or arbitrary source-ID request. Public search remains title/Card Identity summary search without pricing work, price filters, or cost sorting.
- For legacy Published Deck rows, create a public association only from frozen public content and preserved reviewed evidence. Prove Printing separately from Edition. Unproven rows stay readable and use the accepted unpriced/blocked/full-text fallbacks; republish is a new capture, not silent repair.

### Engineered coordination defaults

Extend the existing manifest-oriented public-store adapter boundary with record-oriented operations rather than selecting a vendor, adding per-Deck copies, or adding association sidecars. Trusted database work assigns each Edition a unique stable random 192-bit opaque public reference plus linked public Printing reference; neither derives from an owner or descriptor. Before selecting or executing any existing-Edition identity or compatibility correction—including descriptor cleanup, compatibility keys/aliases, collision resolution, or reference retargeting—complete the actual-target-datastore preflight and reviewed correction plan. Assigning an additive public reference without changing existing identity or references is independent of that correction gate.

Maintain shared record families for public catalog metadata with independent Live and marketplace-verification revisions; USD quotes and confirmed availability keyed by Edition reference plus mapping revision; and source-work admission, coalescing, fence, lease, and backoff state. Use a shared filesystem lock with atomic commit pointer for conditional multi-record commits, and an equivalent linearizable conditional transaction for the HTTP adapter. Ordinary PUT or per-process memory is insufficient. Quote work must not advance the publication manifest or discovery revision. Catalog correction is ordered through a durable outbox: publish quarantine first on contradiction, and let only a newer reviewed correction clear it; delayed replay cannot clear newer invalidation.

Anonymous jobs and public-store reads use zero PostgreSQL access. Reuse the existing bounded on-demand process-launch pattern with public-store ownership, not importer database locks or a periodic sweep. Acquire a shared runner permit before spawning; allow at most two runners globally, each with at most four concurrent source requests under the shared global-eight and per-caller-two limits. A runner exits after two seconds idle and has a supervisor-enforced 65-second maximum lifetime. Expired work is cancelled. Release runner or in-flight source permits only after confirmed completion or cancellation/termination; stale heartbeats or lease expiry alone do not establish that a paused former process has stopped. Uncertain liveness refuses replacement admission and produces labeled fallback. Jobs remain access-triggered and recoverable after launcher failure. Capture a private Deck's complete Holdings at one consistent database revision, release its lock before waiting for a source, and discard a UI result for the wrong revision. Public rows derive only from the active snapshot and revalidate token, version, digest, and current metadata before response; callers cannot submit target IDs, source URLs, quantities, or force requests.

Start the three-second absolute hard-expiry price deadline when the gateway receives the request, for both private and Published Deck price interactions. The gateway establishes trusted budget and caller metadata using the existing server-to-server signing approach; client-supplied deadlines or caller identities cannot extend or replace them. Propagate the remaining budget through admission, adapter reads/conditional writes, source waits, final validation, and response construction. Every blocking operation has a timeout no greater than the remaining budget; the existing relay's five-second timeout cannot be reused for this path. Stop waiting at 2.5 seconds to reserve 0.5 seconds for validation and response. Gate responses with a real monotonic clock, and if authoritative final validation cannot finish, withhold enrichment while the independent snapshot remains available. Deterministic eligibility clocks do not replace measured elapsed-time gateway tests. The following are engineered starting defaults, not measured provider quotas or passed production evidence:

All interactive/source permits, runner permits, queue bounds, token buckets, and caller accounting are enforced atomically through shared admission state across replicas. Derive callers from trusted proxy addresses with a shared rotating keyed digest, consistent across replicas for the accounting interval; keys and raw addresses are never exposed. Refuse new admission at ledger exhaustion rather than evicting active accounting. The caller job cap counts all outstanding admitted work across requests, not a fresh allowance per request.

- Interactive work: 32 global concurrent requests and two per caller; source work: eight global and two per caller.
- Source attempts: 60 per minute with burst eight; queue: 256 global and 32 new jobs per caller. Admit at most 32 new source jobs per access after evaluating all Deck cache rows.
- Schedule callers round-robin and each caller's coalesced work FIFO. A duplicate joins its existing work without a new queue position. Select eligible work by least-recent attempt to make progress beyond the first 32 rows.
- A job lifetime is 60 seconds from first admission, including queue and retry; duplicates do not extend it. Leases last 10 seconds, renew every three seconds, and fence every write.
- Provider work has a two-second total deadline against a fixed allowlisted origin/path, no redirects, and a two MiB response maximum. Allow at most two attempts; transient retry waits 0.5–1 second only while budget remains.
- Backoff begins at 60 seconds, doubles to 15 minutes with 20% jitter, and honors a longer valid Retry-After. Overload reports busy, cached, or unavailable and never advances successful-check age or confirms absence.
- A rendered-row request accepts at most 64 references and 16 KiB body. A public Deck route accepts only token/version and derives rows server-side. Store batches contain at most 128 keys but preserve all-row semantics by chunking.
- The anonymous caller ledger stores a keyed digest only, caps at 10,000 entries, expires after one hour, and stores neither raw IP address nor public view count. Quote/work payloads cap at 250,000 quote entries or 256 MiB; metadata, quarantine, and current-result/supersession markers are durable protected state independent of evictable quote payloads.

A confirmed absence must atomically replace or invalidate the formerly current quote before it is exposed as accepted. If that transition cannot persist, fail closed for the affected Edition across readers and recovery. Cache eviction produces unknown/unavailable state and never searches older retained revisions for fallback. Preserve unexpired negative-cache results and never revive a superseded quote through capacity failure or interrupted writes.

Evaluate the complete cache for every Deck quantity before reporting coverage. If evaluation cannot complete, show “Price unavailable; coverage unavailable; N cards total,” rather than a false `0/N priced` result. Totals and coverage become numeric only after complete evaluation.

Use arbitrary-precision integer quantities serialized as base-10 integer strings. Holding and published-row quantities are positive; aggregate totals may be zero. Validate USD quotes as finite, nonnegative decimal amounts and use exact decimal arithmetic with deliberate final display rounding. The supported-rule matrix covers ordinary Pokémon, Trainer, Special Energy, Basic Energy, promo, subset, ALT, manual, and retired cases only where positive reviewed evidence exists; missing or conflicting inputs are rejected. An ALT without a defensible deterministic rule is unsupported. Do not guess `tcgOnline` or create per-card Live maps. Initially cap a fully percent-encoded Mass Entry URL at 2,000 ASCII bytes as a local usability threshold, not a destination maximum; exceed it by offering complete text. For very large text, provide a bounded-memory download/stream path when clipboard is impractical, without truncating, capping, splitting, or substituting quantities.

Target enablement is independent: Live requires its verified derivation inputs/rules and actual Live acceptance; Mass Entry requires its product/text acceptance; pricing requires exact physical Edition/quote-variant evidence. Failure of one gate does not disable an independently eligible target. Construction warnings and the existing limited-check disclaimer remain visible during export; no tournament-legality claim is added.

## Testing Decisions

- **Confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-08:** use the real application through the real gateway as the primary seam: browser journeys for private/public Deck, card information, publication, and export behavior, plus HTTP contracts/races behind that gateway. Use one focused shared-public-adapter conformance suite only for multi-process compare-and-swap and crash-recovery behavior. These are the highest existing seams; do not add helper-level feature seams.
- Tests assert externally observable behavior: displayed price/age/coverage, complete quantities, export output and fallbacks, public status/visibility, response guards, and database-free anonymous routing. They must not mirror internal helpers or assert private store layout.
- Reuse the established Deck, publication/revocation, discovery, catalog/reconciliation, authenticated browser-workflow, and auth HTTP-harness suites as scenario prior art. Extend those scenarios rather than creating helper-only tests for arithmetic, locks, or serializers.
- Instrument both gateway authentication and API database connections. Public requests with absent, forged, and valid cookies must each prove zero database wakeups; an API-only test client is insufficient evidence of the gateway boundary.
- Use controlled source responses, injected clock, and injected runtime launcher only at external boundaries to exercise check age, provider age, confirmed absence, failures, partial results, hard-expiry wait, outages, and fenced late work. Verify the $1.61 / 5-of-6 example at a user-visible surface.
- Test full Deck behavior with enough Holding pages to prove pricing/export totals do not depend on rendered pagination, and test a concurrent inventory mutation against the consistent server-side read contract.
- Test Live deterministic grouping and blocking across ordinary, promo, subset, Energy, ALT, same-name/different-Printing, manual, retired, unknown, and unsupported examples only where the reviewed rules claim support. Test Mass Entry all-mapped links and complete text fallback, including empty, incomplete, non-60, and oversized Decks.
- Test mapping conflict quarantine, correction precedence, independent Live eligibility, and no stale quote resurrection after confirmed absence or invalidation.
- Test confirmed absence during full storage and interrupted commits: durable supersession must prevent an old quote from appearing in another reader or after recovery. Test finite decimal quotes and positive integer row quantities without fractional-card acceptance.
- Test public paths through the gateway with cookies and without cookies, asserting no session/database access. Exercise publish, republish, revoke, delayed refresh, stale response rejection, durable replay/recovery, and unchanged discovery cursors under quote updates.
- Exercise the focused public-adapter conformance suite with multi-process compare-and-swap, concurrent request, failure, and crash-recovery behavior. Tests must show a late fenced writer cannot overwrite newer public content, revive a revoked token, or clear newer invalidation.
- Capacity acceptance uses at least two API/workers and both public-store adapters. Exercise 60-copy Decks plus 1,000- and 25,000-distinct-Edition Decks; 100 simultaneous callers with overlapping and disjoint Decks; full queues, outage, delayed workers, lease expiry, invalidation, revoke, and republish. Record environment, source concurrency, dedupe, queue age, retries, storage, full-coverage status, and latency.
- Alternate one caller across gateway/API replicas and prove the same two-active-request and 32-outstanding-job caps apply. Test runner crash, pause, failed launch, cancellation, and restart; record actual active processes and client-side outbound requests rather than only lease counts. Exercise slow relay reads and final validation through the real gateway and measure the complete three-second deadline for private and public hard-expiry requests.
- Initial cached ordinary-price views target p95 under 500 ms; an expired result or labeled fallback must complete within three seconds while non-price content remains independent when the upstream is blocked. These are engineered acceptance targets, not passed evidence or a provider-capacity claim.
- The later implementation uses the full regression gate, including `npm run verify`, and records tested source/procedure/environment. It has not been run for this specification.
- The target-datastore preflight and destination/physical/owner walkthrough evidence are launch or correction gates, not substitutes for automated feature tests. Synthetic fixtures do not establish those external proofs.

## Out of Scope

- Feature implementation, migrations, deployed provider selection, correction execution, deployment, commits, and pushes.
- Selecting a deployed provider, executing a correction, or performing the target-datastore preflight, destination, physical-match, walkthrough, deployment, or launch evidence described by the launch checklist.
- A manual user price-refresh control, scheduled sweeping of unaccessed cards, arbitrary anonymous source-ID fetching, or private inventory/session reads on public routes.
- Cheapest-playable-version substitutions, purchase-price bookkeeping, historical price charts, condition/grading valuation, additional currencies, non-English catalog support, deck import, and full tournament-legality claims.
- Universal catalog coverage, universal Live acceptance, finish/condition purchase guarantees, or treating reference comparisons and bounded fixtures as launch evidence.
