## Question

What collector-workspace presentation and selection model makes individual quantities visible and selectable without changing the quantity-based Holding model?

## Conclusion

The owner selected small, separate visual tiles for each quantity unit, minimal workspace controls, and cross-location group selection. The full accepted interaction, responsive rules, and product direction are in the linked resolution. Fan-based temporary copy handles are superseded.

## Scope boundary

The resolution establishes UI/product direction only. Multi-source transaction and Undo design remains open in [atomic multi-source inventory operations](30-decide-atomic-multi-source-inventory-operations.md); it does not modify the current MVP/backend contract.
